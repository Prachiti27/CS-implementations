<h1 align="center">Digital Logic Design</h1>

<p>
This folder contains educational implementations of fundamental
digital logic concepts used to represent and manipulate binary
information in digital systems.
</p>

<p>
The implementations explore Boolean algebra, combinational logic,
sequential circuits, and binary arithmetic to understand how
logical operations form the basis of digital circuit behavior.
</p>
