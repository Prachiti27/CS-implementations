
<h1 align="center">Theory of Computation</h1>

<p>
This folder contains implementations of fundamental theoretical
models used to study computation, formal languages, and
the recognition of structured input.
</p>

<p>
The implementations explore abstract machines, language
recognition, computational models, and the relationships
between formal languages and the systems that process them.
</p>
